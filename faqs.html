Ordering & Products

All our fresh produce and factory-made products are available to order through our official Jumia store. Simply search "Living Fountains Farm" on Jumia or use the "Shop on Jumia" button in our menu.
Yes — every fruit, vegetable and herb on our Our Farm page is grown organically on our Kwale farm, without synthetic shortcuts.
Our factory turns farm produce into honey, sesame oil and toasted sesame oil, sesame seeds, peanut butter, hibiscus petals and powder, granola, mango sorbet and nice cream. See Our Factory for details.
Orders placed through Jumia are fulfilled and delivered via Jumia's own logistics. For bulk, wholesale or direct enquiries, please contact us directly.
