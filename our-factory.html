Our Factory

From harvest to shelf

Our factory team processes fruit and herbs harvested from Our Farm into honey, oils, powders and frozen treats — every batch handled with care.

Inside the factory

Our people, at work

How it works

Handled with care, every step

  1. Ripe fruit and herbs are picked at Our Farm and delivered fresh to the factory.
  2. Our personnel sort, wash and grade every batch — mangoes and passion fruits go straight into processing.
  3. Machines press, blend, dry or freeze the produce into oils, powders, spreads and frozen treats.
  4. Finished products are packaged and listed for sale on our Jumia store.
