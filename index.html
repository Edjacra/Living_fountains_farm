Our Farm

Matunda, Mboga & Mimea ya Dawa — grown organically

  • Fruits like Nanasi (pineapple), Nazi (coconut) and Machungwa (oranges).
  • Vegetables from Sukuma and Managu to Nyanya and Karoti.
  • Herbs and spices including Tangawizi (ginger) and Ufuta (sesame).
